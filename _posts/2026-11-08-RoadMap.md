## Overview

For this algorithms assignment, I implemented a road navigation system that simulates a network of roads and traffic lights. The program finds the fastest route between two locations, taking into account road travel times and traffic light delays.

## Implementation

The assignment involved applying graph-based algorithms and data structures to represent and navigate a road network.

Key concepts included:

- Graph representation and traversal
- Shortest-path algorithms
- Weighted graphs
- Traffic light simulation
- Route construction and travel-time calculations

I also implemented an advanced navigation algorithm that considers road closures when determining the best route.

## What I Learned

This assignment helped strengthen my understanding of graph algorithms and how they can be applied to real-world problems.

I particularly enjoyed seeing how algorithms can be used to model practical systems, such as GPS navigation, where finding the shortest route is not necessarily the same as finding the fastest route.

It also gave me experience working with more complex data structures and implementing algorithms that account for additional constraints.
